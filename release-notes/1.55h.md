# 1.55h

- Before-mode Target Highlight starts with parry feedback and runs for its own duration, independently of hitlag. After mode keeps its two-tick delay after hitlag and an eligible White Flash.
- Target Highlight defaults: Perfect Only On, Before, 6 ticks, 60% world dim and 80% glow. The experimental master switch remains Off by default.
- Hitlag defaults to Perfect Only Off, 6 ticks perfect and 1 tick regular. Tools and shields use 2 perfect ticks within a 4-tick total parry window. Mob timing is unchanged.
- The shield icon is enabled by default. Regular, perfect and blocked-hit icon expansions are off by default; Guard Broken expansion, Parry Ready Lift and echoes remain. Echo growth no longer depends on the icon expansion toggle.
- Added Icon Position under HUD Icons, with enforced HUD defaults: drag placement, nine presets, adjustable HUD gap, Reset, Apply/Cancel, keyboard nudging and hideable controls. Center keeps the original position; custom positions scale with the usable screen.
- Top Center follows visible boss bars. Bottom Center follows shared HUD stacking heights. Hotbar Left/Right avoid the vanilla offhand slot and extra bounds reported by integrations. Independently drawn modded slots need integration or manual placement.
- Enabled/Disabled feature buttons use neutral styling; compact section toggles use green On styling.

Use matching client and server versions. Older presets remain supported, with new position settings filled from defaults. Keeping settings preserves saved values; Reset applies the new defaults.
