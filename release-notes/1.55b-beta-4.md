# 1.55b-beta-4

- Moved highlight target creation out of the living-entity renderer. The callback copies only submitted vertices and retains the original rendering consumer. Mask drawing runs in an isolated post-entity pass.
- Restores framebuffer bindings, viewport, material flags, shader/color/texture state and vertex-buffer cache after offscreen work, including resize and error paths. This addresses the source defect suspected of making a captured mob disappear during hitlag.
- Reuses highlight mask/scratch targets and per-material allocators between nearby parries. Frozen highlights capture once; deferred highlights skip mask work while preceding effects are still playing. Resources release after five idle seconds or session cleanup.
- White Flash starts as hitlag ends. Highlight timing labels are Hitlag and After: Hitlag lasts exactly as long as the freeze; After waits only for White Flash, then uses the configured full lifetime. If no flash plays, After starts at the end of hitlag. Camera Shake and Chromatic Aberration do not delay this sequence.
- Disabled, perfect-only-ineligible and zero-strength screen effects skip their drawing/capture work. White Flash Off also stops Meme Flash. Highlight skips mask work while waiting for the flash; its Duration control appears only in After mode. Active chromatic and impact passes recheck their switches.
- Renamed the perfect hitlag slider to Hitlag Duration. Camera Shake and Chromatic Aberration retain their existing duration labels.
- Config values, packet schema, gameplay, particles, artwork and Punchy playback are unchanged. Protocol remains 72.

Validation: source grammar/declarations/imports/signatures, packet arity/codecs, interpreted highlight transitions and flash queue, capture-request boundaries, render-state restoration and unchanged gameplay/particle/Punchy files. No Java build or game/GPU execution was performed.
